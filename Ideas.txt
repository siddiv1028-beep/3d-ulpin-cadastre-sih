🧮 2. The 4 Core Engines Under the Hood
Engine A: Vertical Parcel Slicing (

core/spatial_math.py
)
The Problem: Traditional land records treat land as a flat 2D polygon ($X, Y$). In high-rises or metro networks, multiple owners share the exact same $(X, Y)$ coordinate at different elevations ($Z$).
How it works:
Given a building with base_elevation ($Z_{base}$) and total_height ($H$), it segments the vertical space into standard floor units (default: $3\text{m}$ per floor).
Superstructure (Above Ground): $$\text{Floor } f: \quad Z_{start} = Z_{base} + (f \times 3), \quad Z_{end} = Z_{base} + ((f + 1) \times 3)$$
Subsurface (Underground Basements): $$\text{Basement } b: \quad Z_{start} = - (b \times 3), \quad Z_{end} = - ((b + 1) \times 3)$$
Every vertical unit gets its own distinct volumetric bounding box.
Engine B: 3D ULPIN Generator & Decoder (

core/ulpin_logic.py
)
Indian Government Standard: The Ministry of Rural Development's Bhu-Aadhaar (ULPIN) is a 14-digit code based on India's Local Government Directory (LGD): $$\underbrace{27}{\text{State}} , \underbrace{21}{\text{District}} , \underbrace{101}{\text{Sub-District}} , \underbrace{050}{\text{Village/Ward}} , \underbrace{0101}_{\text{Plot ID}}$$
Our 3D Extension: We add an ISO 19152 compliant vertical suffix:
Above-ground: -004 (Floor 4) $\to$ 27211010500101-004
Underground: -U02 (Basement 2) $\to$ 27211010500101-U02
Bidirectional Parsing (decode_ulpin): When anyone types or pastes an 18-character code, the system decomposes it into its legal state jurisdiction, plot number, and exact floor level, then instantly commands the camera to swoop to that location.
Engine C: 3D Subsurface Collision & Encroachment Engine (

core/collision_engine.py
)
The Problem: Deep foundation digs and private basements frequently breach underground public utilities (Metro lines, high-pressure pipelines, data conduits).
The Mathematics:
Horizontal Proximity: Computes ground distance ($D$) between coordinates $(lat_1, lon_1)$ and $(lat_2, lon_2)$ using the Haversine formula: $$a = \sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta \phi}{2}\right) + \cos(\phi_1)\cos(\phi_2)\sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta \lambda}{2}\right)$$ $$D = 2R \cdot \arctan2(\sqrt{a}, \sqrt{1-a})$$
Vertical Interval Overlap ($\Delta Z$): $$\text{Overlap}Z = \min(Z{top1}, Z_{top2}) - \max(Z_{base1}, Z_{base2})$$
Classification:
If $D \le (R_1 + R_2)$ and $\text{Overlap}_Z > 0$: 🔴 Critical Direct 3D Encroachment
If $D \le (R_1 + R_2 + 20\text{m})$: 🟡 Safety Buffer Violation (structural risk)
Otherwise: 🟢 Compliant
Engine D: Cryptographic Certificate & Dynamic QR Generator (

core/certificate_generator.py
)
Tamper-Proof Verification: Computes an immutable SHA-256 digital signature: $$\text{Hash} = \text{SHA-256}(\text{ULPIN} \parallel \text{Owner} \parallel \text{Lat} \parallel \text{Lon} \parallel Z_{\text{range}} \parallel \text{Valuation})$$ If anyone modifies the floor number or ownership, the hash immediately invalidates.
Dynamic QR Code: Generates a high-contrast 2D barcode containing the digital verification payload.
ReportLab PDF Engine: Assembles an official Government of India Property Title Deed with tables, digital stamps, and legal disclaimers.
